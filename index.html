What do the numbers mean?
  • Ready – built vehicles at the plant that are free for new orders. This is the number to order against.
  • Free to order (three-wheelers) – three-wheelers not promised to any order. They are kept as kits and assembled when ordered.
  • Kits – vehicles not assembled yet, and where they are: Plant warehouse, 180/C warehouse, Yard, or Main plant. They become “ready” after assembly.
  • On order – dealer orders already waiting for this colour (they take stock first).
  • Total stock – everything at the plant: built vehicles (free + already promised) and kits.
  • Produced / Delivered – vehicles built and sent out, for the latest day and for this month.
  • Built stock comes from the plant’s evening Stock & Delivery report and kits from the Stores vehicle stock report. Both change once a day.
